How it moves

Small, constant life, all of it still when the phone asks for reduced motion.

Hand-drawn boil

The outline redraws with a soft wobble 8 times a second, like a hand-animated line.

Breathing

The body rises and settles every 3.6 s; open smiles breathe with it.

Blinks

Every 2.2 to 5.6 s, sometimes a quick double. Oval eyes squeeze to a line.

Glances and darts

It turns its face to one side now and then, and its eyes dart a little inside the face.

Spin on a mood change

A full turn in 0.75 s with a hop. The new face is swapped in while it looks away; it lands with a squash.

Eyes and mouth apart

The mouth follows a turn a beat later (at most 0.3 rad behind) and sits deeper, so the face is never one rigid stamp.

Talking

When its message changes, the mouth opens and closes about 4 times a second for 1.2 s.

Three-quarter view

Next to a speech bubble it rests turned toward it, so it reads as talking to you.
